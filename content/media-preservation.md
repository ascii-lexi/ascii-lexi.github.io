## Ownership and You
Have you tried buy a piece of digital media lately? A book, a song, a movie, a TV show. You have probably noticed that its very hard to do this in a way that doesn't require an internet connection. Only way that still remains is to own physical media, which can be very inconvenient (almost intentionally so).
